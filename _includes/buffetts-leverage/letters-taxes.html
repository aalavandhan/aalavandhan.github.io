On deferred taxes

In economic terms, the liability resembles an interest-free loan from the U.S. Treasury that comes due only at our election.

Warren Buffett, 1989

In effect, this deferred tax liability is equivalent to a very large transfer tax that is payable only if we elect to move from one asset to another.

Warren Buffett, 1989

The final funding source – which again Berkshire possesses to an unusual degree – is deferred income taxes. These are liabilities that we will eventually pay but that are meanwhile interest-free.

Warren Buffett, 2018

Between now and then, we in effect have an interest-free “loan” that allows us to have more money working for us in equities than would otherwise be the case.

Warren Buffett, 2018
