On borrowed money

Unquestionably, some people have become very rich through the use of borrowed money. However, that's also been a way to get very poor.

Warren Buffett, 2010

When leverage works, it magnifies your gains. Your spouse thinks you're clever, and your neighbors get envious. But leverage is addictive.

Warren Buffett, 2010

History tells us that leverage all too often produces zeroes, even when it is employed by very smart people.

Warren Buffett, 2010

Borrowed money has no place in the investor's tool kit: Anything can happen anytime in markets.

Warren Buffett, 2014

Our aversion to leverage has dampened our returns over the years. But Charlie and I sleep well. Both of us believe it is insane to risk what you have and need in order to obtain what you don't need.

Warren Buffett, 2017

My partner Charlie says there is only three ways a smart person can go broke: liquor, ladies and leverage. Now the truth is — the first two he just added because they started with L — it's leverage.

Warren Buffett, 2018

If your actions are sensible, you are certain to get good results; in most such cases, leverage just moves things along faster. Charlie and I have never been in a big hurry.

Warren Buffett, 1989

When forced to choose, I will not trade even a night's sleep for the chance of extra profits.

Warren Buffett, 2008
